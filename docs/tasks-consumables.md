# Tasks

The witch and warlock classes will have four tasks unique to their class.
Performing tasks in a specific order will result in the player gaining a consumable.
The tasks (t1, ..., t4) required for each consumable (c1, ..., c4) are decided as follows:

```raw
c1 | t1 | t2 |
c2 | t3 | t4 |
	 c3   c4
```

If you perform a row of tasks (t1 and t2/t3 and t4), you get the consumable in that row and likewise for the columns.

## Warlock Tasks

1. Contact patron: stand in front of a etherial shape/body
	- Alternatively, talk on the phone
2. Make sacrifice: kill a goat
3. Pick a rune: pick one from a bunch on a table
4. Channel a spell: stand still and "absorb" some particles


## Witch Tasks

1. Stir cauldron
2. Collect poison berries
3. Pet the black cat
4. Find a clean flask

# Consumables (spells and potions)

## Warlock spells

1. The Awesome Weapon sPell (AWP): get a long-range sniper spell
2. I CAST FIREBALL: wind-up and aoe detonate
3. Summon husk: follows the witch and deals damage on contact
4. Invisibility

## Witch potions

1. Poison: short-range aoe
2. Murder cat: black cat follows the warlock and deals damage on contact
3. Spontaneously detonating potion: trap potion
4. Invisibility
